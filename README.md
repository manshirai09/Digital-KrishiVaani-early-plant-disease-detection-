Digital KrishiVaani is an AI-powered agricultural technology platform designed to help farmers **detect plant diseases at an early stage**, assess crop health risks, and receive actionable agricultural advisory through a simple and farmer-friendly interface.

The platform combines **Computer Vision, Artificial Intelligence, Weather Intelligence, Risk Forecasting, and Multilingual Voice Assistance** to support farmers in making timely and informed crop-management decisions.

---

## 🚜 Problem Statement

Farmers often identify crop diseases and pest infestations only after visible symptoms have spread significantly across the field.

Limited access to agricultural experts, delayed diagnosis, changing weather conditions, and lack of timely advisory can result in:

* Crop yield losses
* Unnecessary pesticide application
* Incorrect disease management
* Increased cultivation costs
* Delayed intervention
* Environmental impact due to excessive chemical usage

Digital KrishiVaani aims to provide an accessible technology-based solution for **early detection, risk assessment, and responsible crop management**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Digital KrishiVaani provides an integrated platform where farmers can:

* 📷 Scan crops using their smartphone camera
* 🦠 Detect potential plant diseases using AI-based Computer Vision
* 🌱 Understand crop health status
* ⚠️ Identify potential crop health risks
* 🌦️ Receive weather-based crop intelligence
* 📊 Get early risk forecasts
* 🎙️ Interact with the VAANI multilingual voice assistant
* 🧑‍🌾 Receive farmer-friendly agricultural recommendations
* 👨‍🔬 Access expert/scientist validation where applicable

The goal is to transform complex agricultural information into **simple, actionable guidance for farmers**.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

### 1. 📷 Crop Health Scanner

Farmers can capture or upload an image of a plant leaf/crop.

The AI model analyzes visible symptoms and provides a potential disease or crop-health diagnosis.

### 2. 🦠 Early Plant Disease Detection

Computer Vision and Deep Learning techniques are used to identify disease symptoms from crop images.

The system is designed to support early identification so that farmers can take preventive or corrective action before the problem spreads.

### 3. ⚠️ Crop Health Risk Diagnostics

The platform evaluates crop-health indicators and categorizes potential risks.

Example:

```text
Low Risk       🟢
Moderate Risk  🟡
High Risk      🟠
Critical Risk  🔴
```

### 4. 📅 7-Day Risk Forecast

Weather and crop-related information can be used to estimate potential crop-health risks over the upcoming days.

This helps farmers prepare for conditions that may favor disease or pest development.

### 5. 🌦️ Weather Intelligence

Weather information can support agricultural decision-making by providing information such as:

* Temperature
* Humidity
* Rainfall
* Wind conditions
* Weather trends
* Disease-favorable conditions

### 6. 🎙️ VAANI – Voice Agricultural Assistant

VAANI is designed as a multilingual voice-based agricultural assistant.

Farmers can interact using voice instead of relying completely on text-based interfaces.

The objective is to make the platform more accessible to farmers with limited digital literacy.

### 7. 🌾 Agricultural Advisory

The platform provides crop-management recommendations based on the detected condition and available agricultural information.

Advisory can include:

* Cultural management
* Mechanical management
* Biological interventions
* Bio-pesticide options
* Responsible chemical guidance

Chemical recommendations should be governed by applicable agricultural regulations and approved product/label information.

### 8. 👨‍🔬 Expert / Scientist Validation

Where required, AI-generated results can be supported by expert validation to improve reliability and reduce the risk of incorrect recommendations.

## 🛡️ Responsible Agricultural Advisory

Digital KrishiVaani is designed around the principle of **responsible crop management**.

The advisory framework prioritizes:

1. Preventive measures
2. Cultural practices
3. Mechanical management
4. Biological interventions
5. Bio-pesticide interventions
6. Chemical intervention only when appropriate and supported by applicable approved guidance

AI-generated information should not replace qualified agricultural experts or official agricultural recommendations in high-risk situations.
